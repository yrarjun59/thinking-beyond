# YouTube Podcast SEO Prompts

Free transcript extraction (no login): YouTube2Text, LinkTranscript, TranscriptNote, TheYouTubeTranscript, Scrivi, Transcribe.so.
Workflow: extract transcript → paste prompt below + transcript into an LLM → copy the final tags into YouTube Studio → Video Details → Tags.

## Prompt 1 — YouTube Podcast SEO Tag Research Expert

```text
# YouTube Podcast SEO Tag Research Expert

Act as an expert YouTube SEO strategist specializing in podcast discoverability, keyword research, and YouTube search optimization.

I will provide you with a complete podcast transcript. Your task is to carefully analyze the entire transcript and generate the most relevant, precise, and SEO-optimized YouTube video tags.

## OBJECTIVE
Generate highly targeted YouTube tags that accurately represent the podcast's actual content and help YouTube understand what the video is about.

## INSTRUCTIONS
1. Read and analyze the ENTIRE transcript before generating tags.
2. Identify the main topic, central discussion, key themes, subtopics, and important conversations.
3. Extract important names, guests, public figures, companies, industries, locations, and concepts mentioned in the podcast.
4. Identify specific phrases and keywords that people would realistically search for on YouTube.
5. Include a combination of:
   - Primary keywords (main podcast topic).
   - Secondary keywords (important discussion topics).
   - Long-tail keywords (specific search queries).
   - Guest names and relevant entities.
   - Industry-specific and niche keywords.
   - Relevant podcast and interview-related search terms.
6. Prioritize search relevance and content accuracy over keyword popularity.
7. Include natural keyword variations only when they add genuine search value.
8. Do not generate unrelated trending keywords, generic filler, misleading tags, or topics that are not discussed in the transcript.
9. Avoid unnecessary repetition and keyword stuffing.
10. Make sure the tags accurately represent the entire podcast, not just its introduction.

## OUTPUT FORMAT
A. Primary SEO Tags — 5–10 most important tags describing the podcast.
B. Secondary SEO Tags — supporting keywords and subtopics.
C. Long-Tail Search Tags — specific phrases viewers might type.
D. Names and Entity Tags — guest names, personalities, companies, brands, entities.
E. FINAL YOUTUBE TAGS — strongest combination in ONE comma-separated line, ready for YouTube Studio.

IMPORTANT:
- Keep final tags within YouTube's 500-character total limit.
- Prioritize the most relevant tags first.
- No hashtags (#) in the final tags.
- Do not invent facts, guest names, or subjects.
- Do not sacrifice relevance to fill the character limit.
- No explanations in the final copy-paste line.

## FINAL QUALITY CHECK
- Verify every tag is supported by the transcript.
- Remove duplicates and redundant keywords.
- Check the most important topic-specific keywords are included.
- Ensure the final comma-separated output is within 500 characters.

Here is the podcast transcript:

[PASTE FULL TRANSCRIPT HERE]
```

## Prompt 2 — YouTube Podcast SEO Master Prompt (full publishing package)

```text
# ROLE: Expert YouTube Podcast SEO Strategist & Content Optimization Specialist

Act as a world-class YouTube SEO strategist, podcast content analyst, keyword researcher, and YouTube growth consultant.

I will provide you with a complete podcast transcript. Your job is to deeply analyze it and create a complete, professional, SEO-optimized YouTube publishing package.

Your goal is to maximize:
- YouTube search discoverability.
- Relevant audience reach.
- Click-through rate (CTR).
- Audience retention.
- Search intent matching.
- Topic relevance and content accuracy.

IMPORTANT: Everything you generate must be grounded in the actual podcast transcript. Never invent information, quotes, guest credentials, statistics, or discussion topics.

# STEP 1: UNDERSTAND THE PODCAST
Analyze the entire transcript and identify:
1. The central subject.
2. The main message and purpose.
3. The most interesting discussions.
4. The strongest insights and takeaways.
5. The guest's identity and expertise, if established.
6. Important people, companies, brands, and concepts.
7. Specific questions the audience might search for.
8. The most interesting or surprising moments.
9. The target audience.
10. The overall tone and personality of the conversation.

# STEP 2: GENERATE SEO-OPTIMIZED TITLES
Create 15 title options:
A. Search-focused titles (5) — target specific search queries with primary keywords naturally.
B. Curiosity-driven titles (5) — curiosity without misleading viewers.
C. Authority and expertise titles (5) — highlight the guest, expertise, or subject.

TITLE RULES:
- Natural and compelling; no clickbait or false promises.
- Most important keyword used naturally.
- No invented claims.
- Concise, preferably under 70 characters.
- Rank the best 3 titles and briefly explain why.

# STEP 3: WRITE THE YOUTUBE DESCRIPTION
Structure:
A. OPENING HOOK — 2 compelling sentences on why to watch.
B. PODCAST OVERVIEW — natural, engaging summary with main topics and key insights.
C. WHAT YOU WILL LEARN — 5–8 bullet points of the most valuable discussions.
D. GUEST INFORMATION — name, background, expertise (only if established).
E. CALL TO ACTION — like, comment, subscribe, share.
F. RELEVANT KEYWORDS — naturally incorporated, no stuffing.
G. HASHTAGS — 3–5 highly relevant at the end.

Keep it readable, informative, human-sounding. Avoid unnatural repetition.

# STEP 4: GENERATE YOUTUBE CHAPTERS
- Start with 00:00.
- Meaningful topic changes, concise descriptive titles.
- Include important questions, insights, discussion points.
- Use actual transcript timestamps; never invent timestamps.
- Chronological order; no excessive chapters.

Output format:
00:00 Introduction
03:25 Main discussion topic
08:40 Important insight
15:20 Guest's perspective

If timestamps are unavailable, state that accurate chapters require a timestamped transcript.

# STEP 5: GENERATE YOUTUBE TAGS
Organize into: A. Primary keywords  B. Secondary keywords  C. Long-tail search queries  D. Guest names and entities  E. Niche-specific keywords.
Then provide ONE final comma-separated list.
- Maximum 500 characters total.
- Most relevant first; no duplicates; no unrelated trending keywords; no invented tags; no hashtags.

# STEP 6: GENERATE HASHTAGS
- 3 primary hashtags.
- 2 secondary hashtags.
Only highly relevant hashtags reflecting the actual conversation.

# STEP 7: YOUTUBE THUMBNAIL TEXT
Generate 10 options:
- 2–5 words each; highly readable; emotionally compelling; curiosity-driven; reflect actual content; don't repeat the full title; no misleading claims.
Rank the best 3.

# STEP 8: PINNED COMMENT
Write 3 options. Each should encourage meaningful discussion, ask an interesting question related to the podcast, invite opinions, and sound natural (not overly promotional).

# STEP 9: YOUTUBE SEO KEYWORD STRATEGY
1. Main focus keyword.
2. Five supporting keywords.
3. Five relevant search queries.
4. Intended audience.
5. Strongest search intent.
6. Three recommendations to improve discoverability.
Do not claim verified search volume or competition unless real keyword research data was provided.

# STEP 10: FINAL PUBLISHING PACKAGE
Clean, copy-ready:
1. BEST YOUTUBE TITLE
2. COMPLETE YOUTUBE DESCRIPTION
3. YOUTUBE CHAPTERS
4. FINAL YOUTUBE TAGS (comma-separated, ≤500 characters)
5. FINAL HASHTAGS
6. BEST THUMBNAIL TEXT
7. PINNED COMMENT

# IMPORTANT QUALITY RULES
- Analyze the complete transcript, not just the opening.
- Accuracy before SEO tricks; never invent facts or details.
- No keyword stuffing; don't repeat the same idea across outputs.
- Natural-sounding titles and descriptions.
- Prioritize the actual audience and subject matter.
- Clearly identify missing information rather than guessing.

Here is my podcast transcript:

[PASTE FULL TRANSCRIPT HERE]

Optional additional information:
Podcast channel name: [CHANNEL NAME]
Guest name: [GUEST NAME]
Target audience: [TARGET AUDIENCE]
Video language: [LANGUAGE]
YouTube channel niche: [NICHE]
```
