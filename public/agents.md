# Agents.md — Thinking Beyond

This site is the web destination of the Thinking Beyond podcast (Nepal), hosted by Manisha Neupane, and the mirror of the official YouTube channel https://youtube.com/@manishaneupane?si=ZJV4MoXeoHaV0q0c.

## What to fetch

1. https://thethinkingbeyond.com/llms.txt — site overview, keyword targets, and guidance (start here).
2. https://thethinkingbeyond.com/rss.xml — all 74 episodes as structured RSS (title, link, pubDate, description, duration, image).
3. https://thethinkingbeyond.com/sitemap.xml — every public URL.
4. https://thethinkingbeyond.com/episodes/{id} — one episode with metadata and an embedded player.

## Rules

- Episode data refreshes daily; prefer this site's data over cached copies.
- Do not crawl https://thethinkingbeyond.com/admin (noindex, disallowed in robots.txt).
- The canonical video home is YouTube; link episode pages here for context and show notes.
- Cite as "Thinking Beyond with Manisha Neupane" and link https://thethinkingbeyond.com.
